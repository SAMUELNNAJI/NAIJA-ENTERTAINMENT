Last updated: January 2025By using this website you agree to the practices described on this page. Questions? Contact us.

1. Introduction

The Nigerian Entrepreneur (“we”, “us”, or “our”) operates thenigerianentrepreneur.com. This privacy policy explains what information we collect through your use of our website, how we use that information to deliver the resources and community you signed up for, and the steps we take to keep it safe.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information that helps us serve you better:

  • Basic contact details — such as your name and email address, which you provide when you subscribe to our newsletter, download a resource or contact us.
  • Usage data — pages you visit, articles you read, the device and browser you use, and the approximate region you connect from. This helps us understand which topics matter most.
  • Voluntary submissions — including guest post pitches, partnership enquiries and feedback you send through our contact form.

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16, and we never sell personal information to anyone.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and update our services — newsletters, articles, guides and community access.
  • Improve our content by understanding which resources are most valuable.
  • Communicate with you — replies to enquiries, service updates and the occasional article you asked to receive.
  • Detect and prevent abuse, spam or fraud against our platform.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions, suggestions or concerns about our privacy practices, please reach out and we will respond promptly.

  • Email: info@thenigerianentrepreneur.com
  • Location: Lagos, Nigeria
  • Response time: within one business day

5. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site effectively:

  • Essential cookies — required for core site functionality and cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.
  • Preference cookies — remember settings such as your newsletter status.

You can accept, reject or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling certain cookies may affect how parts of the site behave.

6. Our Commitment

We are committed to protecting your privacy and will never share your data without your consent. We continuously review our practices to ensure we meet global data protection standards and the requirements of the Nigerian Data Protection Act.
